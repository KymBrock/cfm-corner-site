[Mosiah 25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/25?lang=eng)

Mosiah 25 details the gathering of the Nephite people in Zarahemla under King Mosiah II after the reunion of Limhi&#x27;s people and Alma&#x27;s followers with the Nephites in Zarahemla. This chapter emphasizes the importance of unity, recounts the various journeys and trials of Limhi’s and Alma’s groups, and describes the establishment of the church under Alma’s leadership. Key themes include the power of collective faith, the importance of righteous leadership, and the blessings of communal worship. The chapter highlights significant events and spiritual lessons, connecting them to broader scriptural themes, providing insights for contemporary application.

References and Cultural Contexts for Investigation, Contemplation, and Discussion:

- Unity and Collective Faith: The chapter emphasizes the importance of unity among different groups of people coming together under shared faith and leadership.

- People: Descendants of Nephi, Mulekites, 1st Mosiah&#x27;s followers, Alma&#x27;s followers, the people of Limhi, the children of Amulon

- Records: Brass Plates, Plates of Nephi, Records of Zeniff,  24 Golden Jaredite Plates, Interpreters (Urim & Thummim), etc. 

- Righteous Leadership: Highlights the role of righteous leaders like Alma the Elder in organizing and guiding the church.

- Communal Worship and Repentance: Describes the establishment of the church and the collective commitment to living gospel principles.

Verses 1-11: Gathering and Recounting of Histories

1-2: King Mosiah II gathers all the people together in Zarahemla.

- Cultural Context: The significance of communal gatherings in ancient societies for worship, maintaining unity, and cultivating a shared identity. The [Moedim](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4150/kjv/wlc/0-1/), מוֹעֵד "appointed feast days" 

3-4: The people of Zarahemla consist of Nephites, people of Zarahemla (Mulekites), and those who came with Limhi and Alma.

- Narrative Development: The integration of various groups into one community.

5-6: King Mosiah reads the [records](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=account&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc) of Zeniff’s people and Alma’s group to the gathered [assembly](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=assembled&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc).

- Theological Insight: The importance of preserving and recounting historical records to maintain collective memory and faith, also to stand as a witness for God, that his prophecies were fulfilled.

7-9: The people are filled with joy, wonder, amazement, and sorrow as they listen to the accounts of deliverance and suffering.

- Biblical Cross-Reference: Similar to the reading of the Law by Ezra in [Nehemiah 8:1-9](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/neh/8/1-9/s_421001), where the people respond with both joy and weeping.

10-11: The people recognize the hand of God in their deliverance and are moved to raise their voices in praise, gratitude, and worship.

- [Has An Artifact That Relates to the Book of Mormon Been Found?](https://knowhy.bookofmormoncentral.org/knowhy/has-an-artifact-that-relates-to-the-book-of-mormon-been-found)

- The [Moedim](https://news.kehila.org/what-are-the-moedim/), "appointed/fixed feast days, times, or seasons". "The word moedim doesn’t solely mean a festive time or a season of celebration. It is also marinated in remembering and reflecting on all of the times God showed Himself mighty and strong, loving and holy."

- Moedim as observed in Scripture: [Gen 1:14](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/gen/1/14/s_1014), [Leviticus 23](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/lev/23/2/s_113002), [Alma 40:10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/40?lang=eng), [D&C 121:30-32](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/40?lang=eng), [Abraham 3:4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/abr/3?lang=eng)

Verses 12-24: Alma’s Leadership and the Establishment of the Church

12-13: King Mosiah allows Alma the Elder to organize the church among the people. The children of Amulon and his brethren (Noah&#x27;s wicked priests) forsake the names of their fathers and change their names to Nephites.

- Theological Insight: The role of righteous leadership in spiritual organization and governance, and the consequences of unrighteous dominion.

14-15: Alma preaches repentance and faith in Jesus Christ to the people.

- Cultural Context: The continuity of prophetic teaching and its impact on the community.

16-18: Many people are [baptized](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g907/kjv/tr/0-1/) and join the church, indicating a communal commitment to live according to gospel principles.

- Biblical Cross-Reference: The mass baptisms following Peter’s sermon on the Day of [Pentecost](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4005/kjv/tr/0-1/) ([Shavuot](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h7620/kjv/wlc/0-1/) the counting of 7 "Weeks") in [Acts 2:37-41](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/act/2/37-41/s_1020037).

- [Miqvah](https://www.chabad.org/theJewishWoman/article_cdo/aid/1541/jewish/The-Mikvah.htm), gathering/collection of waters ([מִקְוֶה](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4723/kjv/wlc/0-1/) Gen 1:10, Lev 11:36) This link on Jewish traditions associated with Miqvah&#x27;s adds some interesting perspectives. While there are some distinctions that differ from Christian or LDS theological doctrine,there are many valuable insights that one can glean from examining the Jewish perspective, especially as one examines Alma&#x27;s experience, as it was based on Jewish tradition, prior to the fulfillment of the Law through Jesus Christ.

19-20: Alma [ordains](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=ordain&t=KJV&lexcSt=0#s=s_lexiconc) [priests](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=priest&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc) and [teachers](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=teacher&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc) / [rabbis](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4461/kjv/tr/0-1/) to lead and instruct the people, ensuring the church&#x27;s proper organization and functioning.

- Theological Insight: This passage highlights the importance of priesthood authority and proper organization in maintaining the church&#x27;s spiritual integrity. Greek word for Church: ἐκκλησία [Ekklesia](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1577/kjv/tr/0-1/) or συναγωγή [synagōgḗ](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4864/kjv/tr/0-1/) Hebrew word: קָהָל [qâhâl](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h6951/kjv/wlc/0-1/), or מוֹעֵד [môwʻêd](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4150/kjv/wlc/0-1/)

21-22: The church prospers and grows under Alma’s leadership, experiencing peace and unity.

- Narrative Development: The blessings of collective faith and righteousness.

23-24: The people of Zarahemla and those from the land of Nephi live in harmony, unified in their faith and worship.

- Cultural Context: The significance of unity and communal worship in strengthening societal bonds.

###### [Mosiah 26](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/25?lang=eng)

Mosiah 26 addresses the challenges that arise within the Nephite church as it expands. This chapter explores issues of apostasy, disciplinary actions within the church, and the importance of divine revelation in resolving complex spiritual matters. Key themes include trials of church leadership, the necessity of revelation, the process of repentance, and the importance of maintaining the purity of the church.

References and Cultural Contexts for Investigation, Contemplation, and Discussion:

- The Key Doctrine of the Resurrection: 

- [The Doctrine of Resurrection in the Book of Mormon](https://archive.bookofmormoncentral.org/content/doctrine-resurrection-book-mormon)

- The Healing Potential of Discipline: Encourages modern readers to support and embrace  church discipline to support growth and integrity, both individually and within one&#x27;s community.

- Embracing Repentance: Inspires individuals to seek repentance and forgiveness, understanding the transformative power of these principles.

- Seeking Divine Guidance: Highlights the importance of seeking divine guidance in making difficult decisions, both individually and collectively.

- Dealing with Challenges: Provides insights on how to address challenges within faith communities with compassion, firmness, and reliance on divine instruction.

- Literary and Linguistic Insights:

Mosiah 26

Verses 1-6: The Challenges of Apostasy

1-2: A new generation arises that does not believe in the traditions of their fathers and begins to lead others astray and [persecute](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=persecute&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc) the church.

- Cultural Context: The impact of generational shifts on religious belief and practice, where not all who join a faith community remain faithful, or those in positions of authority, through pride and abuse of power, lose the trust of the faith community.

3-4: Many are deceived and commit sin, leading to significant [apostasy](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g571/kjv/tr/0-1/) "lack of [faith](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4103/kjv/tr/0-1/)/trust" within the church.

- Theological Insight: The vulnerability of faith without proper teaching/leadership, and the importance of maintaining spiritual foundations and purity.

5-6: The leaders of the church, including Alma, are troubled by the increasing wickedness and seek guidance. Dissension arises, leading to disputes and divisions within the church.

Verses 7-14: Alma Seeks Divine Guidance

7-8: Alma, distressed by the situation, brings those who have sinned before King Mosiah, who refuses to judge them. 

- Theological Insight: Mosiah&#x27;s refusal to judge the unrepentant discenters is indicative of the separation of ecclesiastical and civil authority according to Jewish law.

9-10: Alma turns to the Lord for guidance on how to handle the situation.

- Biblical Cross-Reference: Similar to Moses seeking guidance from God in [Numbers 27:1-5.](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/num/27/1-5/s_144001)

11-12: Alma prays fervently for divine direction and understanding as he examines the details of the situation, pouring out his whole soul to God.

- Theological Insight: The importance of seeking divine wisdom in leadership. 

13-14: The voice of the Lord comes to Alma, providing revelation on how to address the issue of apostasy with love and compassion.

- [Why Does the Book of Mormon Say We Must Worship God with Our Whole Soul?](https://knowhy.bookofmormoncentral.org/knowhy/why-does-the-book-of-mormon-say-we-must-worship-god-with-our-whole-soul)

Verses 15-31: Divine Revelation and Church Discipline

15-20: The Lord, in his instructions to Alma, blesses him for his faith and diligence in teaching the people and bringing them to God. Alma receives his [calling](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=called&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc) and [election,](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=elect&t=KJV&lexcSt=0#s=s_lexiconc) and is promised eternal life. He is instructed that those who follow Alma&#x27;s example and take upon themselves the name of Christ, willingly [bearing](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=bear&t=KJV&lexcSt=0#s=s_lexiconc) His budens in diligent service and loving compassion for the welfare of the transgressor will also be counted as His and will receive the same blessings of the eternal covenant.  Alma receives his Divine commision, to love and serve the repentant sinner. The Lord instructs Alma to appropriately discipline those who have committed sin, emphasizing the role of charity and maintaining spiritual integrity and purity within the Church.

- Theological Insight: These sections enforce the importance of church discipline in preserving the integrity of the faith community.  

- Hebraic Insight: The concept of [nacham](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5162/kjv/wlc/0-1/) (נָחַם) and [teshuvah](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8666/kjv/wlc/0-1/) (תשובה), two very different aspects of repentance, and central to Jewish tradition are emphasized here.

- Take up my Yoke ([Matt 11:29](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/mat/11/29/s_940029))

- Take up My Cross, and Follow Me ([Matt 16:24](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/mat/16/24/s_945024))

17-20: The Lord reassures Alma of his authority and responsibility as the leader of the church.

- Biblical Cross-Reference: Similar reassurances given to Joshua in [Joshua 1:5-9](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/jos/1/5-9/s_188005).

- Theological Context: This section highlights the doctrine of repentance as a means of loving reconciliation with God, bearnign similarities to the Old Testament sacrifices for atonement ([Leviticus 5:5-6](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/lev/5/5-6/s_95005)).

- Biblical Cross-Reference: Jesus’ teaching on forgiveness in [Matthew 18:21-22](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/mat/18/21-22/s_947021)

21-23: Alma receives his Divine commision, he is instructed to gather and feed God&#x27;s sheep, preaching repentance and faith in Jesus Christ to all.

- Theological Insight: The central role of repentance and the Atonement in church teachings. Word for Atonement in Greek: [katallage](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g2643/kjv/tr/0-1/) καταλλαγή , and in Hebrew: [kippur](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3725/kjv/wlc/0-1/) כִּפֻּר or [kafar](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3722/kjv/wlc/0-1/) כָּפַר

24-26: Those who repent and confess their sins will be [forgiven](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=forgive&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc) and accepted back into the church, while those who reject Christ, refuse the power and healing of his redemption. "I am their [Redeemer](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1350/kjv/wlc/0-1/); but they would not be [redeemed](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=redeem&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc)"

- Biblical Cross-Reference: Parallels to the parable of the prodigal son in [Luke 15:11-32](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/luk/15/11-32/s_988011).

In the Hebrew language, that covenantal love is called hesed (חֶסֶד).
Hesed has no adequate English equivalent. Translators of the King James Version of the Bible must have struggled with how to render hesed in English. They often chose “lovingkindness.” This captures much but not all the meaning of hesed..."

- [Counting the Omer - Week 1: The Attribute of Loving-Kkindness (Chesed)](https://www.chabad.org/multimedia/video_cdo/aid/1498662/jewish/Counting-the-Omer-Week-1.htm)

Verses 4-8: King Mosiah’s Response and Preparation

4-5: King Mosiah inquires of the Lord about his sons’ mission, and the Lord assures him that they will be protected and successful in their efforts.

- Cultural Context: Seeking divine guidance before making significant decisions is a common theme in the Bible (1 Samuel 23:2, 4).

6-7: The Lord promises that he will deliver Mosiah&#x27;s sons out of the hands of the Lamanites, King Mosiah trusts the Lord, blesses his sons, and sends them on their mission, demonstrating his faith and support for their endeavors, according to their [request](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=request&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc).

8: The sons of Mosiah prepare to depart, leaving behind their responsibilities and privileges as heirs to the kingdom.

Verses 9-20: The Transmission of the Kingdom and the Sacred Records

9-10: King Mosiah, concerned about the preservation of sacred [records](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=record&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc), inquires of the Lord and decides to confer the records upon Alma the Younger. (Plates: לוּחַ [lûwach](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3871/kjv/wlc/0-1/))

- Biblical Cross-Reference: Similar to Moses conferring leadership to Joshua ([Deuteronomy 31:7-13](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/deu/31/7-13/s_184007)).

11-12: The records include those written by Mosiah, Alma, and other Nephite leaders, as well as the plates of brass and the 24 gold plates found by the people of Limhi.

- Hebraic Insight: The importance of written records and genealogy ([יָחַשׂ yâchas](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3187/kjv/wlc/0-1/),[ ](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8435/kjv/wlc/0-1/)[תּוֹלְדָה tôwlᵉdâh)](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8435/kjv/wlc/0-1/)[ ](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8435/kjv/wlc/0-1/) in maintaining cultural identity and religious purity ([1 Chronicles 9:1](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/1ch/9/1/s_347001)).

13-14: Alma the Younger is entrusted with the sacred records, emphasizing his role as the spiritual leader and [record](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=record&t=KJV#s=s_lexiconc) [keeper](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=keeper&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc) ([scribe](https://www.blueletterbible.org/search/search.cfm?Criteria=scribe&t=KJV&lexcSt=2#s=s_lexiconc)).

15-16: King Mosiah translates the Jaredite plates using the Urim and Thummim, revealing the history and destruction of the Jaredites.

- Cultural Insight: The use of the Urim and Thummim for divine revelation and translation (Exodus 28:30).

17-19: The account of the Jaredites serves as a warning to the Nephites about the consequences of wickedness and the importance of adhering to God’s commandments.

20: King Mosiah finishes his reign by emphasizing the importance of the records and their preservation for future generations.
