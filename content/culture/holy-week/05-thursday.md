## The Day

**Date:** Thursday, 14 Nisan (March/April)

**Location:** Upper Room (Mount Zion) → Gethsemane (Mount of Olives)

**Key Events:**
- Preparation of the Passover meal
- Jesus washes the disciples' feet
- The Last Supper — institution of the Sacrament
- Identification of the betrayer
- The New Commandment: "Love one another"
- The Farewell Discourse (John 14–16)
- The Intercessory Prayer (John 17)
- Gethsemane — the Atonement begins
- Betrayal and arrest

## Word Studies

### Gethsemane — גַּת שְׁמָנִים

<a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1068/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="oil press" data-strongs="G1068" data-translit="Gethsēmanē" data-original="Γεθσημανῆ" data-lang="greek">Γεθσημανῆ</a> (*Gethsēmanē*) — from Hebrew <a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1660/kjv/wlc/0-1/" target="_blank">גַּת</a> (*gath*, "press") + <a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8081/kjv/wlc/0-1/" target="_blank">שֶׁמֶן</a> (*shemen*, "oil")

**Meaning:** "Oil press" — the place where olives were crushed to extract oil.

The symbolism is profound: Just as olives are crushed to produce oil — used for light, healing, and anointing — so Christ was "crushed" under the weight of our sins to bring light, healing, and anointing to the world.

---

### Maundy — mandatum

The name "Maundy Thursday" comes from the Latin *mandatum* (commandment), referring to Jesus' "new commandment":

> "A new commandment I give unto you, That ye love one another; as I have loved you."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/13?lang=eng&id=34" target="_blank">John 13:34</a>

---

## The Setting of the Last Supper

**Location:** An upper room on Mount Zion, likely in a wealthy home

**The Table:** A triclinium — a U-shaped Roman-style table adopted by Jews. Guests reclined on cushions around the table.

**Seating Arrangement:**
- Jesus sat second from the left (not at the center as often depicted)
- John, "the beloved disciple," reclined next to Jesus
- Judas was likely seated in a position of honor, demonstrating Jesus' unconditional love even toward His betrayer

## The Atonement in Gethsemane

### The Agony

> "And being in an agony he prayed more earnestly: and his sweat was as it were great drops of blood falling down to the ground."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/22?lang=eng&id=44" target="_blank">Luke 22:44</a>

### Book of Mormon Witness

> "He shall go forth, suffering pains and afflictions and temptations of every kind... he will take upon him the pains and the sicknesses of his people... that his bowels may be filled with mercy."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/7?lang=eng&id=11-12" target="_blank">Alma 7:11–12</a>

### Doctrine and Covenants Witness

> "Which suffering caused myself, even God, the greatest of all, to tremble because of pain, and to bleed at every pore, and to suffer both body and spirit."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/19?lang=eng&id=18" target="_blank">D&C 19:18</a>

## Key Insights from Video Research

**The Last Supper:**
> "The blood of the Passover lamb symbolizes redemption from slavery; Christ's blood = redemption from sin."

**Gethsemane:**
> "Christ's atoning sacrifice is likened to the process of pressing olives — the crushing weight of sin He endured."

**Scriptures Referenced:** <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/12?lang=eng&id=1-13" target="_blank">Exodus 12:1–13</a>; <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/26?lang=eng&id=26-29" target="_blank">Matthew 26:26–29</a>; <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/14?lang=eng&id=32" target="_blank">Mark 14:32</a>; <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/22?lang=eng&id=39" target="_blank">Luke 22:39</a>; <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/18?lang=eng&id=1" target="_blank">John 18:1</a>

## Reflection Questions

1. Jesus washed Judas's feet, knowing what was coming. How does this challenge your understanding of love?

2. In Gethsemane, Jesus asked the Father to remove the cup, "nevertheless not my will, but thine, be done." When have you had to surrender your will to God's?

3. Jesus chose an olive press — a place of crushing — for His great atoning work. What does the symbolism of Gethsemane mean to you?
