## The Day

**Date:** Friday, 15 Nisan (March/April)

**Location:** Jerusalem — from Caiaphas's house to Pilate's court to Golgotha

**Key Events:**
- Trial before Caiaphas and the Sanhedrin
- Peter's denials
- Trial before Pilate; sent to Herod
- Barabbas released; Jesus condemned
- Scourging and mocking
- The Via Dolorosa (way of suffering)
- Crucifixion at Golgotha (9 AM – 3 PM)
- The seven last words from the cross
- Death of Jesus (3 PM)
- Temple veil torn; earthquake
- Burial in Joseph's tomb

---

## Timeline of Good Friday

| Time | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2–4 AM | Jesus brought before Annas, then Caiaphas |
| Dawn | Formal Sanhedrin condemnation |
| ~6:30 AM | First hearing before Pilate |
| ~7:00 AM | Sent to Herod |
| ~8:00 AM | Returned to Pilate; Barabbas released |
| ~9:00 AM | Crucifixion begins (third hour) |
| 12–3 PM | Darkness covers the land |
| ~3:00 PM | Death of Jesus (ninth hour) |
| ~4:00 PM | Burial in Joseph's tomb |
| Sunset | Passover Sabbath begins |

## Key Insights from Video Research

**The True Lamb of God:**
> "Jesus is the 'true Lamb of God' — unjustly judged by those who judged sacrificial lambs. At the precise moment that Jesus died on the cross, around 3:00 PM, the temple lambs were being offered for sacrifice."

**What Was Crucifixion Like?**
> "Crucifixion involved severe flogging with a whip embedded with glass, bone, and lead. The sacrament bread symbolizes Christ's torn flesh. Jesus could have been killed by stoning or by one of many other ways but he instead chose to be crucified."

**The Trial:**
> "Pilate declared Jesus innocent but yielded to public pressure. Truly it is by his blood coming upon us that we are forgiven."

## Reflection Questions

1. Why is this day called "Good" Friday? What is good about it?

2. Jesus cried, "My God, why hast thou forsaken me?" What does this moment of divine withdrawal mean for our salvation?

3. "It is finished." What was finished? How does this affect your life today?
