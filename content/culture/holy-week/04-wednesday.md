## The Day

**Date:** Wednesday, 13 Nisan (March/April)

**Location:** Jerusalem (Caiaphas's palace) and Bethany

**Key Events:**
- Chief priests and elders conspire at Caiaphas's palace
- Judas Iscariot agrees to betray Jesus
- Thirty pieces of silver — the price of betrayal
- An unnamed woman anoints Jesus (some traditions)
- Jesus rests in Bethany

## Word Studies

### Thirty Pieces of Silver — τριάκοντα ἀργύρια

<a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5144/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="thirty" data-strongs="G5144" data-translit="triakonta" data-original="τριάκοντα" data-lang="greek">τριάκοντα</a> (*triakonta*) — thirty

<a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g694/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="silver" data-strongs="G694" data-translit="argurion" data-original="ἀργύριον" data-lang="greek">ἀργύριον</a> (*argurion*) — silver coin

**The Price of a Slave:** According to <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/21?lang=eng&id=32" target="_blank">Exodus 21:32</a>, thirty shekels of silver was the compensation paid for a slave gored by an ox. Judas valued the Son of God at the price of a slave.

**Prophetic Fulfillment:** This exact amount was foretold in <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/zech/11?lang=eng&id=12-13" target="_blank">Zechariah 11:12–13</a>: "They weighed for my price thirty pieces of silver... And I took the thirty pieces of silver, and cast them to the potter in the house of the LORD."

---

### Betray — παραδίδωμι

<a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3860/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="to hand over, deliver up" data-strongs="G3860" data-translit="paradidōmi" data-original="παραδίδωμι" data-lang="greek">παραδίδωμι</a> (*paradidōmi*) — to hand over, deliver up, betray

This word means more than "betray" — it means to **deliver** or **hand over**. The same word is used of the Father "delivering" His Son for us all (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/rom/8?lang=eng&id=32" target="_blank">Romans 8:32</a>). What Judas meant for evil, God used for redemption.

## The Conspirators

### Caiaphas

**Joseph Caiaphas** served as high priest from 18–36 AD — the longest tenure of any high priest during Roman rule. He was son-in-law to Annas, the former high priest who still wielded significant power behind the scenes.

### The Sanhedrin

The ruling council met to plan Jesus' arrest, but they feared the crowds:

> "Not on the feast day, lest there be an uproar among the people."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/26?lang=eng&id=5" target="_blank">Matthew 26:5</a>

### Judas Iscariot

Why did Judas betray Jesus? Theories include:
- **Greed** — he was a thief who kept the money bag (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/12?lang=eng&id=6" target="_blank">John 12:6</a>)
- **Disappointment** — Jesus wasn't the political Messiah he expected
- **Satanic influence** — "Satan entered into Judas" (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/22?lang=eng&id=3" target="_blank">Luke 22:3</a>)

Whatever his motivation, Judas's later remorse suggests he didn't fully understand the consequences of his actions.

## Theological Significance

### The Shadow of the Cross

Wednesday is sometimes called "Spy Wednesday" because of Judas's agreement to spy out an opportunity to betray Jesus. The shadow of the cross grows darker.

### Divine Sovereignty

Even in the conspiracy, God's plan unfolds. Jesus would die at exactly the right moment — as the Passover lambs were being sacrificed — fulfilling centuries of prophecy.

---

## Latter-day Saint Connections

### The Atonement Foreshadowed

The anointing recorded in Matthew and Mark (whether the same event as <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/12?lang=eng&id=1-8" target="_blank">John 12</a> or a separate occurrence) connects to sacred temple anointing. The woman prepared Jesus' body for burial, just as members prepare through sacred ordinances.

### Agency and Accountability

Judas exercised his agency to betray, yet his choice was anticipated by prophecy. This illustrates the profound truth that God's foreknowledge does not eliminate human choice or accountability.

## Reflection Questions

1. Judas valued Jesus at thirty pieces of silver. What "price" do we sometimes put on our loyalty to Christ?

2. The priests wanted to arrest Jesus "not on the feast day." When have you seen people try to serve God on their own terms?

3. Luke says "Satan entered into Judas." How do we guard against subtle spiritual influence that leads us away from Christ?
