The Tabernacle was the *mishkan* (מִשְׁכָּן), the dwelling place God commanded Israel to build so that He could dwell among His covenant people. This field guide treats the Tabernacle not only as a structure, but as a teaching system in sacred space, ritual movement, color, fabric, fire, and stone.

Use the field guide in layers. Begin with the map to see how the sanctuary was ordered from gate to veil, then move into the priestly garments to see how Israel’s representative quite literally bore the sanctuary’s theology on his body.
