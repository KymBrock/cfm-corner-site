# Sources and Further Reading

This page gathers the sources behind the three parts of *Roots, Branches, and Light*. Each part links its sources where they are used and ends with a short list of works cited; here they are all in one place, grouped by kind, with a link and a line on what each one supports.

A word on how the evidence is weighed. Scripture anchors the reading. Later commentators, ancient and medieval, are evidence of *their own* interpretations, not proof of what earlier biblical writers intended. Dictionaries, studies and museum records each have their own role. And the modern teaching chart at the center of the series shows how Hebrew is taught today; it cannot date ancient grammar.

## Scripture

- **The King James Bible, the Book of Mormon, the Doctrine and Covenants and the Joseph Smith Translation**, quoted from the Church's editions at [churchofjesuschrist.org](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures?lang=eng). Every scripture reference in the series links to its verse.
- **Joseph Smith Translation, [John 1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/jst/jst-john/1?lang=eng).** Part 3: the Word tied to the gospel, to life and light, and to the Son (verses 4, 14 and 16).
- **The Hebrew and Aramaic text**, through [Bible Hub](https://biblehub.com/)'s interlinear and text analysis for [Genesis 1:1](https://biblehub.com/interlinear/genesis/1-1.htm), [Isaiah 11:1](https://biblehub.com/text/isaiah/11-1.htm), [Isaiah 29:13](https://biblehub.com/text/isaiah/29-13.htm), [Daniel 4:15](https://biblehub.com/text/daniel/4-15.htm) and [Zechariah 2:2](https://biblehub.com/text/zechariah/2-2.htm), and through [Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org/Proverbs.4.24) for Proverbs 4:24. Parts 2 and 3: the small words and word forms the English does not show.

## Latter-day Saint Resources

- **"[The Menorah](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/ensign/2018/12/the-menorah?lang=eng)," *Ensign*, December 2018.** Part 3, notes: the menorah's treelike form, the burning bush and the tree of life in Jewish tradition.
- **Daniel C. Peterson, "[Nephi and His Asherah](https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/jbms/vol9/iss2/4/)," *Journal of Book of Mormon Studies* 9, no. 2.** Part 1: Leon Yarden's proposal that the menorah represents a stylized almond tree.
- **Jeffrey M. Bradshaw, "[Moses Sees the Garden of Eden (Moses 3): The Symbolism of the Tree of Knowledge and the Tree of Life](https://interpreterfoundation.org/book-of-moses-essays-058)," Book of Moses Essays, no. 58, Interpreter Foundation.** Part 1: whether the lampstand may evoke an oil-bearing tree of life.

## The Menorah, the Tree and the Menorah Psalm

- **Cornelis Houtman, "[Menorah — Lampstand](https://sahd-online.com/words/mnorah/)," *Semantics of Ancient Hebrew Database*, 2013, updated 2025, sections 6.1–6.3.** Part 1: the lampstand's botanical form, and why scholars still disagree about how much it owes to sacred-tree imagery.
- **Philo of Alexandria, *Who Is the Heir of Divine Things?* [sections 215–220](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/philo/works.xix.xliv.html)** (C. D. Yonge translation). Part 3: a first-century Jewish reading of the lampstand as a picture of how God orders the world.
- **Gregory E. Sterling, "[Philo of Alexandria](https://academic.oup.com/reference/62365/reference-article-abstract/554616285)," *Oxford Bibliographies in Classics*, 2015.** Part 3, notes: Philo's dates.
- **Babylonian Talmud, [Bava Batra 25b:8](https://www.sefaria.org/Bava_Batra.25b.8).** Part 3: the rabbinic link between the menorah and wisdom.
- **Bahya ben Asher, commentary on [Exodus 25:31](https://www.sefaria.org/Rabbeinu_Bahya,_Shemot.25.31.4), paragraph 4.** Part 3: the lampstand's 22 cups matched to the 22 letters.
- **Dunash ibn Tamim, [commentary on *Sefer Yetsirah*, chapter 1](https://www.posenlibrary.com/entry/commentary-book-creation-sefer-yetsirah-1)**, translated by Jeffrey G. Amshalem, Posen Library (selections). Part 3: letters, creation and the Abrahamic tradition in a tenth-century commentary.
- **A. Peter Hayman, *Sefer Yeṣira: Edition, Translation and Text-Critical Commentary* (Mohr Siebeck, 2004), [introduction, pp. 1–2](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783161587955_A40613273/preview-9783161587955_A40613273.pdf).** Part 3: the modern edition's view of how commentary became mixed into the text.
- **Abudarham, Weekday Prayers, [Post Amidah 43](https://www.sefaria.org/Abudarham,_Weekday_Prayers,_Post_Amidah.43).** Part 3: Psalm 67 already called the Menorah Psalm, its seven verses matched to seven branches.
- **Sarit Shalev-Eyni, "[Gebetbuch, jüdisch](https://www.rdklabor.de/wiki/Gebetbuch,_j%C3%BCdisch)" (Jewish Prayerbook), *RDK Labor*, 2014, section II.A.2.** Part 3: the dated 1397 Pisa prayer book with Psalm 67 written as a lampstand.
- **David Arnow, "[Jacob's Hope for Salvation: The Extraordinary Career of Genesis 49:18](https://zeramim.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/JacobsHopeforSalvation.pdf)," *Zeramim* IV/3 (2020), pp. 9–10.** Part 3: confirms the 1397 example.
- **Eep Talstra and Carl J. Bosma, "[Psalm 67: Blessing, Harvest and History: A Proposal for Exegetical Methodology](https://etcbc.github.io/bhsa/articles/2001_TalstraBosma_Psalm67.pdf)," *Calvin Theological Journal* 36 (2001): 290–313.** Part 3: the psalm's mirrored structure.
- **National Library of Russia, [First Leningrad Pentateuch](https://expositions.nlr.ru/eng/ex_manus/firkovich/sobr_second.php), Evr. II B 17 (copied 929).** Part 3: the menorah among the tabernacle furnishings in a tenth-century Hebrew manuscript.
- **Later Jewish interpretation of the letter *nun*:** Aaron L. Raskin, "[Nun](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/137086/jewish/Nun.htm)," Chabad.org. Part 1: *nun* as a symbol of continuing life.

## Hebrew Roots, Patterns and Words

- **David Kimhi (Radak), *Sefer HaShorashim*:** the [introduction](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_HaShorashim%2C_Introduction.6) and the entries [גזע](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_HaShorashim%2C_%D7%92%D7%96%D7%A2) (stump) and [שרש](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_HaShorashim%2C_%D7%A9%D7%81%D7%A8%D7%A9%D7%81) (root), on Sefaria. Part 2: tree vocabulary and verb-pattern analysis in the same medieval work.
- **William Chomsky, *[David Kimhi's Hebrew Grammar (Mikhlol)](https://archive.org/details/mikhlol)*, systematically presented and critically annotated.** Part 2: the medieval order of the verb patterns.
- **The Ben-Yehuda dictionary, entry [*binyan*](https://benyehuda.org/dict/24412/39205).** Part 2: *binyan* as "building", and Kimhi's columns of verb forms.
- **The Academy of the Hebrew Language**, terminology records for [root](https://terms.hebrew-academy.org.il/munnah/12660_1), [stem](https://terms.hebrew-academy.org.il/munnah/11102_1/) and [noun pattern](https://terms.hebrew-academy.org.il/munnah/4499_1/%D7%9E%D6%B4%D7%A9%D7%81%D6%B0%D7%A7%D6%B8%D7%9C), and its [rules for verbs by root type](https://eng.hebrew-academy.org.il/our-work/language-decisions/grammar/). Part 2: the meaning of each grammatical term.
- **Brown–Driver–Briggs, *A Hebrew and English Lexicon of the Old Testament***, entries [learn](https://biblehub.com/bdb/3925.htm), [measure](https://biblehub.com/bdb/4058.htm) and [לָמַד, L–M–D](https://biblehub.com/hebrew/3925.htm). Part 2: *learn*, *teach* and *be taught*, and the separate root for measuring.
- **Ernest Klein, *A Comprehensive Etymological Dictionary of the Hebrew Language*, entry [נוּן](https://www.sefaria.org/Klein_Dictionary,_%D7%A0%D7%95%D6%BC%D7%9F.1)**, on Sefaria. Part 1: the letter name *nun* and words for sprouting and offspring.
- **The Blue Letter Bible lexicon** (Strong's numbers with Gesenius), entries for [*shoresh*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8328/kjv/wlc/0-1/), [*binyan*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1146/kjv/wlc/0-1/), [*gezaʿ*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1503/kjv/wlc/0-1/), [*bayit*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1004/kjv/wlc/0-1/), [*mishkal*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4948/kjv/wlc/0-1/), [*et*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h853/kjv/wlc/0-1/), [*katav*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3789/kjv/wlc/0-1/), and the Greek [*logos*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3056/kjv/tr/0-1/), [*pneuma*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4151/kjv/tr/0-1/), [*manthanō*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3129/kjv/tr/0-1/) and [*mathētēs*](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3101/kjv/tr/0-1/). All three parts: the words behind the popups.
- **Mishnah [Peah 1:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Peah.1.1?lang=bi) and [Pirkei Avot 1:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Pirkei_Avot.1.1?lang=bi).** Part 2: *talmud* as study and *talmidim* as disciples.
- **"[Grammar, Hebrew](https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/6854-grammar-hebrew)," *Jewish Encyclopedia*.** Part 2: the history of Hebrew grammar.
- **National Library of Israel, [Leningrad Codex record](https://www.nli.org.il/he/discover/manuscripts/hebrew-manuscripts/itempage?docId=PNX_MANUSCRIPTS990001516230205171&scope=KTIV&vid=KTIV).** Part 2: the oldest complete dated Hebrew Bible (1008–09).
- **Paul Joüon, *Grammaire de l'hébreu biblique* (2nd ed., 1947), [the page on pronunciation](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3APaul_Jo%C3%BCon_-_Grammaire_de_l%E2%80%99h%C3%A9breu_biblique%2C_2e_%C3%A9d.%2C_1947.djvu/29)**, on Wikisource. Part 2: how biblical Hebrew was pronounced.
- **Pantelidis, "[The Greek Alphabet](https://www-old.phil.uoa.gr/fileadmin/phil.uoa.gr/uploads/linguistics/Pdf/papers/Pantelidis_2014_The_Greek_Alphabet___Scriptura_Mundi.pdf)" (2014),** and the **Berlin-Brandenburg Academy's Greek lexicon, [*manthanō*](https://cld.bbaw.de/lemma/grc/%CE%BC%CE%B1%CE%BD%CE%B8%CE%AC%CE%BD%CF%89).** Part 2: how the Greeks adapted the alphabet, and why the learn/measure comparison stays a question.
- **"[Hebrew for the Rest of Us](https://bsg.billmounce.com/GRU_Appendix%20%28Hebrew%29.pdf)," the Hebrew appendix to William D. Mounce, *Greek for the Rest of Us*.** Part 1: a plain introduction to Hebrew verb forms.

## Further Reading

A few works from the research behind the series that it does not link directly:

- **Steven Fine, "[Menorah, Its 'Branches' and Their Cosmic Significance](https://www.thetorah.com/article/menorah-its-branches-and-their-cosmic-significance)," TheTorah.com.** A readable overview of how ancient Jewish writers explained the lampstand's seven lights.
- **[Numbers Rabbah 14:4](https://www.sefaria.org/Bamidbar_Rabbah.14.4).** A rabbinic teaching that words of wisdom take root in a person, close in spirit to Alma's seed.
- **Corbin T. Volluz, "[A Study in Seven: Hebrew Numerology in the Book of Mormon](https://byustudies.byu.edu/article/a-study-in-seven-hebrew-numerology-in-the-book-of-mormon)," *BYU Studies Quarterly* 53, no. 2 (2014).** Patterns of seven in the Book of Mormon. These are literary patterns, not verb forms.
