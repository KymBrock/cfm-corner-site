A compilation of audio and visual resources to assist individuals and families with their Come Follow Me study.

## Weekly CFM Commentary

- **[Scripture Central](https://scripturecentral.org/come-follow-me)** — Previously Book of Mormon Central. The umbrella for several excellent sub-series including CFM Insights with Taylor & Tyler, Finding Christ in the Old Testament, Lynne Hilton Wilson, and Faith Insights. Leading LDS scholars including Tyler Griffin (associate dean, BYU Religious Studies) and Taylor Halverson (Ph.D. in Judaism & Christianity in Antiquity). Excellent for well-researched, accurate doctrine.

- **[Follow Him](https://followhim.co/)** — Hosted by Hank Smith and John Bytheway. Weekly expert interviews. Transcripts and references at followhim.co. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/c/FollowHimOfficialChannel/featured)

- **[Don't Miss This](https://www.dontmissthisstudy.com/)** — Hosted by Emily Belle Freeman and David Butler. Lighthearted, fun presentations for individuals and families. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCDM4cG9T5jvdV3SeHT6ILCw)

- **[Teaching with Power](https://www.youtube.com/c/TeachingwithPower)** — Ben Wilcox, 20+ year seminary and institute veteran. Excellent lessons for teachers, well-organized and scripture-based.

- **[The Scriptures Are Real](https://www.youtube.com/@TheScripturesAreReal)** — Hosted by Kerry Muhlestein (BYU professor and OT scholar) and Lamar Newmeyer. 414+ episodes. Academic rigor with personal connection.

- **[Scripture Insights](https://www.youtube.com/@ScriptureInsightsLearning)** — Weekly scripture insights and learning.

- **[Finding Christ in the Old Testament](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLhfh21X9suLeJgxN1WpXSDs5RFKCU4601)** — John Hilton III (Scripture Central). Weekly Christ-centered messages in OT passages. ~15-20 min videos. New for 2026.

- **[Lynne Hilton Wilson](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLhfh21X9suLdFq2rbJ5yhk-gqqNWauzgx)** — Dr. Lynne Hilton Wilson (Scripture Central). Ph.D. in Theology and American Religious History. Co-founder of Scripture Central. Deep doctrinal and cultural context.

- **[Faith Insights](https://www.youtube.com/@FaithInsights.ScriptureCentral)** — Tyler Griffin (Scripture Central). Quick 5-10 minute faith-building insights each week.

- **[Scripture Gems](https://www.youtube.com/@brotherfullmer)** — Visual scripture summaries and illustrations.

- **[Talking Scripture](https://www.youtube.com/@TalkingScripture)** — Hosted by Mike Day and Bryce Dunford (university-level institute teachers). Focus on relevance and application.

- **[Unshaken](https://www.youtube.com/@Unshaken)** — Youth-focused, making scripture relevant for younger audiences. ~10-15 min videos.

## Podcasts & Audio

- **[Meridian Magazine](https://latterdaysaintmag.com/podcast/)** — Scott and Maurine Proctor. Award-winning writers for Music and the Spoken Word and other LDS publications.

- **[A Restored View of the Old Testament](https://soundcloud.com/scripturecentral/sets/a-restored-view-of-the-old)** — Dr. Lynne Wilson and John Cho. Produced specifically for OT study — especially relevant for OT 2026. Deep doctrine of the Old Testament and how it relates to modern day. (Scripture Central)

- **[Come Follow Me Read-Along](https://podcast.app/come-follow-me-read-along-p702678/)** — Simply a reading of CFM passages by Bradley Ross using the New English Translation (NET). [netbible.org](https://netbible.org/)

## Community & Academic

- **[BYU Religious Education Podcasts](https://rsc.byu.edu/podcasts)** — Wide range of religious and academic topics.

- **[Messages of Christ](https://www.youtube.com/@messagesofchrist)** — Daniel Smith. Essential videos on Ancient Israelite culture including festivals, temple worship, archaeology, geography of the Holy Land, and how Jewish traditions testify of Christ. Excellent companion to OT study.

- **[The Bible Project](https://bibleproject.com/)** — Not LDS but excellent. Used at BYU Jerusalem Center. 41 book overview videos, plus theme videos (Image of God, Heaven & Earth, Tree of Life, Sacrifice & Atonement) and Hebrew word studies (Nephesh/Soul, Ruakh/Spirit, Khesed/Loyal Love, Shalom/Peace, YHWH/LORD). [OT Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLH0Szn1yYNeeVFodkI9J_WEATHQCwRZ0u)

- **[Old Testament Minute (Scripture Central)](https://scripturecentral.org/archive/books)** — Chapter-by-chapter scholarly commentary. 36+ chapters covering Moses through Exodus. Quick, accessible academic insights.

- **[Pearl of Great Price Central](https://www.pearlofgreatpricecentral.org/)** — Dedicated scholarly resources for Moses and Abraham — essential for early OT 2026 weeks.

- **[BYU Easter Conference](https://rsc.byu.edu/conferences/easter)** — Annual conference with General Authority emeritus keynotes, focused on the Savior.

- **[FAIR](https://www.fairlatterdaysaints.org/)** — Well-documented answers to criticisms of LDS doctrine, belief, and practice.

- **[The Interpreter Foundation](https://interpreterfoundation.org/come-follow-me/old-testament-2026/)** — Extensive scholarly resources for OT 2026 including 90+ Book of Moses essays by Jeffrey M. Bradshaw, KnoWhy OTL articles, and weekly lesson resources.
