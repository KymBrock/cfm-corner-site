## Weekly Insights

The question at Mount Carmel names the whole week: "How long halt ye between two opinions? if the Lord be God, follow him: but if Baal, then follow him." Israel's crisis was not only idolatry but indecision. The people had learned to live with divided worship, divided loyalty, and divided kingdoms. Elijah's ministry forces the hidden choice into the open: the God who can shut heaven, feed a widow, raise the dead, answer by fire, and speak in silence is not one option among many. He is Lord.
