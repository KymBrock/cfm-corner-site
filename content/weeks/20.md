## Weekly Insights

Moses is about to be taken from Israel. He knows it. So he preaches the most important sermon of his life — the Shema, the prosperity warning, the Year of Release, the prophet prophecy, and the starkest invitation in scripture: "I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life."
