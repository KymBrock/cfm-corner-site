## Weekly Insights

This week's reading brings us the plagues and the Passover—the defining events of Israel's deliverance. We spent Easter exploring Christ as the Passover Lamb. Now we return to the original narrative, seeing how the symbols of deliverance came to be.

Watch for the women who made the Exodus possible: Shiphrah and Puah who defied Pharaoh, Jochebed who placed Moses in the basket, Miriam who watched and spoke up, Zipporah who acted decisively. The deliverer needed delivering first.

Notice the blood—how *dam* connects to *adam* and *adamah*, how it painted the letter of life over each doorway. And see the doorpost become an altar, the home become a temple, the father become priest. Every threshold crossed in faith echoes that Passover night.
