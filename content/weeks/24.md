## Weekly Insights

Israel crosses a threshold it cannot uncross. They demand a king — and God gives them one. What follows is one of the most tragic arcs in Scripture: a man who had every advantage and lost everything because he could not bring himself to obey. Alongside that tragedy, a shepherd boy emerges from a Bethlehem pasture to become the most important king in Israel's history — chosen not for what he looked like, but for what he believed.
