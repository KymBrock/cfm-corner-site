## Weekly Insights

This week begins with a departure and never stops giving. Elijah is taken up in a whirlwind, the mantle falls, and Elisha strikes the Jordan — "Where is the Lord God of Elijah?" — and the water parts. What follows is one of scripture's richest catalogues of mercy: oil that fills every borrowed jar until the jars run out, a dead child raised, a foreign general healed by washing seven times, a hillside full of chariots of fire disclosed to a frightened servant, and an enemy army fed a feast instead of the sword. Elisha heals lepers, raises the dead, multiplies bread, and loves his enemies — centuries before Jesus does the same in the same land. The mantle falls to the next hand, and the God behind it keeps meeting people exactly where they are, with a provision shaped to the size of the vessel they are willing to bring.
