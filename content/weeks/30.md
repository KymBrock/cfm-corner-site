description: "Week 30 gathers four kings of Judah — Asa, Jehoshaphat, Uzziah, and Hezekiah — and reads their reigns through the Chronicler's one relentless question: when the crisis comes, on whom will you lean? Asa begins by casting himself entirely on God against an overwhelming army, then hires Syria and dies seeking physicians rather than the Lord. Jehoshaphat, facing a coalition he cannot beat, prays 'we have no might… but our eyes are upon thee,' and is told the battle is not his but God's — Judah wins it by sending the choir out ahead of the army. Uzziah is 'marvellously helped, till he was strong,' and the moment he is strong his heart is lifted up to his destruction. The week ends in mercy, as Hezekiah's Passover honors the prepared heart over the perfect procedure and God heals the people."
quick_summary: "Week 30 asks where your weight actually rests when the multitude appears on the horizon. Jehoshaphat's deliverance defines the theme — 'the battle is not yours, but God's… stand ye still, and see the salvation of the Lord' — while Asa's drift from trust and Uzziah's God-given strength curdling into pride mark the warnings. The study guide traces six Hebrew words that carry the week: darash (to seek) and sha'an (to lean), chazaq (to be strong) and gavah (to be lifted up), and the pair that names the rescue and its reward, yeshuah (salvation) and simchah (joy)."
