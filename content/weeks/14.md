## Weekly Insights

Easter is not an interruption in the Old Testament year. It is the moment the whole covenant story has been moving toward.

This week we read Isaiah's promise that God will swallow up death in victory alongside the Gospel witness of the empty tomb, Paul's firstfruits theology, and the Restoration witness of the risen Christ at Bountiful. Elder Gary E. Stevenson invited Latter-day Saints to make 3 Nephi 11 part of our Easter tradition. That invitation shapes this week: the Resurrection is not only announced, it is encountered.

As you study, watch the movement from Sinai to Bountiful. At Sinai the people stood afar off. At Bountiful the risen Christ says, "Arise and come forth unto me." Easter is the hinge that makes that reversal possible.
