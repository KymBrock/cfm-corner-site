## Weekly Insights

Moses is gone — taken by the Lord. Joshua now leads Israel across the Jordan into the land promised to Abraham. The crossing echoes the Red Sea but demands something new: the priests must step into the water before it parts. Wet feet precede the miracle. Jericho falls through worship, not warfare. Rahab — the outsider — sees what Israel's own people often miss. And at Shechem, Joshua forces the question that defines everything: "Choose you this day whom ye will serve." This is the hinge on which the entire Old Testament turns — and the foundation of the pride cycle that will consume the book of Judges.
