# Future Field Guides — Prep Scaffold

Groundwork for the next three Culture field guides, continuing the chronological arc that
runs Solomon's Temple → Divided Kingdom → **Assyria → Babylon → Persia/Greece**. Same house
approach throughout: the four-layer discipline (**[BIBLICAL] / [RECON] / [INTERP] / [LDS]**),
"deepens, not debunks," every verse checked against the local ScriptureDatabase, every named
scholar real and verified before it ships, Moedim visual style, self-owned or PD/CC images only.

Status: **outline only.** No pages built. Sources below are *starting points to verify during
the build*, not finished citations.

Kings" page). Guide C carries the Second Temple forward to the doorstep of the New Testament.

## GUIDE A — Assyria: "The Scattering"

**Thesis.** The empire that erased the ten northern tribes also, by its own policy of mass
deportation, created the "lost tribes" — and Judah survived by a hair. The same records that
condemned the North (the Deuteronomist) now narrate its end; Assyria's own reliefs and annals
let us hear the other side of the story.

**Chronological scope.** Tiglath-Pileser III's campaigns → fall of Samaria 722 BC (Shalmaneser V
/ Sargon II) → deportation & resettlement policy → Sennacherib's 701 BC invasion of Judah →
Hezekiah's deliverance.

**Proposed pages**
1. **Overview — the Assyrian war machine.** Who they were, the terror, the deportation policy.
2. **The Fall of Samaria (2 Kings 17).** The theological autopsy — *why* the North fell.
3. **Where the Ten Tribes Went.** Deportation/resettlement; the origin of the "lost tribes"; the
   Samaritan remnant (ties to the DK guide's new Samaritan section).
4. **Judah's Near Miss — Sennacherib & Hezekiah (2 Kings 18–19; Isaiah 36–37).** Lachish, the
   siege of Jerusalem, Hezekiah's tunnel (already illustrated in the First Temple guide).
5. **The Other Side of the Story (interactive).** Assyrian reliefs & annals vs. the biblical
   account — the Lachish reliefs, Sennacherib's Prism, the Black Obelisk.
6. **Reading Faithfully / The Gathering.** [LDS] the doctrine of the gathering of Israel and the
   lost tribes — hope on the far side of scattering.

**Scripture spine.** 2 Kings 15–19; Isaiah 7, 10, 36–37; Hosea & Amos (northern prophets on the
eve); Micah 1. [LDS] 3 Nephi 15–16 / D&C 110:11 (gathering); Article of Faith 10.

**Sources to pursue (verify during build).** ANET / *Sennacherib's Prism (Taylor/Oriental
Institute Prism)*; the **Lachish reliefs** (British Museum); the **Black Obelisk of Shalmaneser
III** (Jehu); the **Nimrud** palace reliefs; the Kurkh Monolith. Scholarship: standard Assyrian
histories (e.g. Grayson's Assyrian royal inscriptions); LDS gathering scholarship via IF/RSC.

**Visuals.** Deportation relief scene (Moedim); a scattering map (tribes → Assyrian provinces);
Lachish siege scene; reuse Hezekiah's-tunnel photo/asset from First Temple. Licensing note: BM
relief photos vary — favor own photos, PD, or Moedim originals.

## GUIDE B — Babylon: "The Fall"

**Thesis.** The house Solomon built comes down. The catastrophe of 586 BC is the very event the
Deuteronomist was writing *toward* — and it is the hinge that the whole Book of Mormon opens on,
since Lehi leaves Jerusalem in its shadow (~600 BC). Exile is not the end of the covenant; it is
where much of the Hebrew Bible was compiled.

**Chronological scope.** Rise of Babylon (Nabopolassar/Nebuchadnezzar) → Carchemish 605 →
first deportation 597 (Jehoiachin) → siege and destruction of Jerusalem & the First Temple 586 →
Gedaliah → exile → Jehoiachin's release ~561 (the note that ends Kings).

**Proposed pages**
1. **Overview — Babylon rising.** Nebuchadnezzar, the neo-Babylonian empire, the countdown.
2. **The Destruction of the First Temple (2 Kings 25; 2 Chron 36).** The burning of the house;
   what was lost; the vessels carried off. (Direct sequel to the First Temple guide.)
3. **The Prophets of the Collapse — Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Lamentations.** The voices in the rubble;
   "by the rivers of Babylon" (Psalm 137).
4. **Lehi in the Countdown.** [LDS] the ~600 BC departure; the brass plates; ties straight to the
   Divided Kingdom guide's "Book of Mormon & Kings" page.
5. **Exile & the Making of the Book (interactive).** How catastrophe became canon — the
   Deuteronomistic History finalized; the archaeology of the destruction (City of David burnt
   layer, the bullae — already in the First Temple guide).
6. **Reading Faithfully — Covenant in the Ashes.** [LDS] the "scattering and gathering" pattern;
   Jeremiah's new-covenant promise (Jer 31).

**Scripture spine.** 2 Kings 24–25; 2 Chronicles 36; Jeremiah (esp. 39, 52, 31); Lamentations;
Ezekiel 1, 8–11 (the glory departs), 37; Psalm 137; Daniel 1. [LDS] 1 Nephi 1–7; Omni; Mosiah 1
(brass plates).

**Sources to pursue (verify during build).** The **Babylonian Chronicles** (ABC 5 / Jerusalem
Chronicle, BM 21946); the **Lachish Letters** (ostraca); the **Jehoiachin ration tablets**
(Babylon); the City-of-David destruction evidence (Reich/Shukron, E. Mazar — already cited in
First Temple guide). Scholarship: standard exile histories; Barker/Christensen thread for the
pre-exilic brass-plates argument (already vetted in the DK guide).

**Visuals.** Burning-temple scene (Moedim); "glory departs" (Ezekiel) figure; a deportation-route
map; Psalm-137 riverside scene. Reuse First-Temple City-of-David assets.

## GUIDE C — Persia & Greece: "Return, and the Coming of the Greeks"

**Thesis.** Cyrus the Persian lets the exiles home and the Second Temple rises — then, two
centuries later, Alexander sweeps away the Persian (Achaemenid) empire and remakes the whole
Near East in Greek. The world the New Testament opens in — Hellenized, Greek-speaking, temple
rebuilt — is forged in this gap.

**Chronological scope.** Cyrus's decree 539 → return under Zerubbabel, Second Temple 516 →
Ezra & Nehemiah → the Achaemenid century → the Greco-Persian Wars (Marathon 490, Thermopylae/
Salamis 480) as the backdrop → Alexander's conquest of Persia 334–330 → the Diadochi
(Ptolemies/Seleucids) → Hellenization → Maccabean revolt 167–160 → to the edge of Rome/Herod.

**Proposed pages**
1. **Overview — the Persian peace.** The Achaemenid empire; Cyrus as the LORD's "anointed"
   (Isaiah 45:1); the policy that reversed Babylon's.
2. **The Return & the Second Temple (Ezra–Nehemiah; Haggai; Zechariah).** The humbler house
   (ties to the First Temple guide's Zerubbabel timeline node); "the glory of this latter house."
3. **Achaemenid Persia vs. Greece.** The Greco-Persian Wars — the collision of two worlds
   (the specific "Achaemenid dynasty and Greece" thread the guide is asked to carry).
4. **Alexander & the Greek Flood.** Conquest 334–330; Hellenization; the Septuagint (Greek OT);
   Greek as the language the NT would be written in.
5. **The Maccabean Crisis (interactive).** Antiochus IV, the abomination, the revolt, Hanukkah;
   Daniel's visions read against it.
6. **Reading Faithfully — the Stage Is Set.** [LDS] how the Persian return + Greek diffusion
   prepared the world for Christ; the Second Temple as the one Jesus walks into.

**Scripture spine.** 2 Chronicles 36:22–23; Ezra; Nehemiah; Haggai; Zechariah; Isaiah 44:28–45:1
(Cyrus); Daniel 2, 7, 8, 11 (the empires). (Deuterocanon for context: 1–2 Maccabees.)
[LDS] the Second Temple as backdrop to the NT; Septuagint & textual transmission (Article of
Faith 8 again).

**Sources to pursue (verify during build).** The **Cyrus Cylinder** (BM); the Behistun
Inscription; the Elephantine papyri (a Jewish community in Persian Egypt); standard Achaemenid
histories (e.g. Briant, *From Cyrus to Alexander*); Hellenistic-Judaism scholarship; the
Septuagint. Herodotus for the Greco-Persian wars (with the usual source-critical caution).

**Visuals.** Cyrus-Cylinder photo (PD/BM); Second-Temple-rising scene (reuse/adapt First-Temple
assets — it's the *same platform*); a Persian-empire and an Alexander-empire map; a Maccabean/
Hanukkah scene. The Second Temple visuals can share DNA with the First Temple guide.
