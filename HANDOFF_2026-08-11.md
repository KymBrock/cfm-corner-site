## 6. Safety rules (from CLAUDE.md — non-negotiable)

- **Never batch-regenerate** weekly HTML; targeted single-file edits only; ≤3 week files per commit
  (pre-commit hook blocks >5). Always `git diff` before committing.
- **Content integrity**: every claim sourced (scripture / vault / distilled transcript / verified talk /
  Kymber's direct instruction / approved grammar refs). Never hand-author pointed Hebrew or characterize a
  video/talk from memory. When unsure, leave a flagged placeholder.
- **Never flip `current:` flags** except as an actual deployment step with approval.
- Voice = accessible general-LDS: clear, scriptural, source-disciplined, not academic. Link every Hebrew term
  and every scripture citation (full book + chapter, normalized `data-ref`).
