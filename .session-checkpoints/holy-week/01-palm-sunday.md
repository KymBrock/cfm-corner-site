## The Day

**Date:** Sunday, 10 Nisan (March/April)

**Location:** From Bethany, over the Mount of Olives, into Jerusalem

**Key Events:**
- Jesus sends disciples for a colt
- The Triumphal Entry into Jerusalem
- Crowds wave palm branches and shout "Hosanna!"
- Jesus weeps over Jerusalem
- Jesus teaches Greek seekers

## Word Studies

### Hosanna — הוֹשִׁיעָה נָּא

<a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5614/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="save, we pray" data-strongs="G5614" data-translit="hōsanna" data-original="ὡσαννά" data-lang="greek">ὡσαννά</a> (*hōsanna*) — from Hebrew <a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h3467/kjv/wlc/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="to save, deliver" data-strongs="H3467" data-translit="yasha" data-original="יָשַׁע" data-lang="hebrew">יָשַׁע</a> (*yasha'*, "save") + <a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4994/kjv/wlc/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="now, I pray" data-strongs="H4994" data-translit="na" data-original="נָא" data-lang="hebrew">נָא</a> (*na*, "please/now")

**Meaning:** "Save now!" or "Save, we pray!"

This cry comes from <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ps/118?lang=eng&id=25-26" target="_blank">Psalm 118:25–26</a>, part of the Hallel psalms sung at Passover:

> "Save now, I beseech thee, O LORD... Blessed be he that cometh in the name of the LORD."

The crowds were explicitly identifying Jesus as the Messianic King promised by the prophets.

---

### Palm Branches — φοίνιξ / תָּמָר

<a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5404/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="palm tree, palm branch" data-strongs="G5404" data-translit="phoinix" data-original="φοίνιξ" data-lang="greek">φοίνιξ</a> (*phoinix*) — palm branch

Palm branches were associated with **Sukkot** (Feast of Tabernacles), not Passover. The Hebrew word <a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h8558/kjv/wlc/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="palm tree" data-strongs="H8558" data-translit="tamar" data-original="תָּמָר" data-lang="hebrew">תָּמָר</a> (*tamar*) appears in <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/lev/23?lang=eng&id=40" target="_blank">Leviticus 23:40</a> as part of Sukkot celebration.

By waving palm branches at Passover, the crowds were conflating two festivals — both pointing to the Messiah. Sukkot celebrates God dwelling with His people; Passover celebrates deliverance. Jesus fulfills both.

## Prophecy Fulfilled

> "Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion; shout, O daughter of Jerusalem: behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/zech/9?lang=eng&id=9" target="_blank">Zechariah 9:9</a>

Jesus deliberately arranged this fulfillment. He sent disciples to find a specific colt, knowing exactly where it would be. This was not coincidence — it was covenant.

**Why a donkey?**

Kings rode horses in war and donkeys in peace. Solomon was placed on David's mule to signal his coronation (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-kgs/1?lang=eng&id=33" target="_blank">1 Kings 1:33</a>). Jesus entered as a **King of Peace** — not the military conqueror many expected.

## Latter-day Saint Connections

### The Hosanna Shout

The "Hosanna Shout" performed at temple dedications directly echoes Palm Sunday. Members wave white handkerchiefs (symbolic palm branches) and shout:

> "Hosanna, Hosanna, Hosanna to God and the Lamb!"

This connects modern Saints to those who welcomed Jesus into Jerusalem — acknowledging Him as King.

### The Temple and the King

Just as Jesus entered Jerusalem and went directly to the Temple (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/11?lang=eng&id=11" target="_blank">Mark 11:11</a>), Latter-day Saints understand that the Lord's return will involve His temple:

> "The Lord, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/mal/3?lang=eng&id=1" target="_blank">Malachi 3:1</a>

Palm Sunday foreshadows the Second Coming.

## Reflection Questions

1. The crowds shouted "Hosanna" — "Save now!" What do you need the Savior to save you from today?

2. Jesus wept over Jerusalem because "thou knewest not the time of thy visitation" (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/19?lang=eng&id=44" target="_blank">Luke 19:44</a>). How can we be more attentive to the Lord's presence in our lives?

3. Jesus entered as a King of Peace, not war. How does this shape your understanding of His kingship?
