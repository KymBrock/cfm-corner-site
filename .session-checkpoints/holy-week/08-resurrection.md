## The Day

**Date:** Sunday, 17 Nisan (March/April) — The First Day of the Week

**Location:** Joseph's tomb, Jerusalem, Emmaus

**Key Events:**
- Earthquake; angels roll away the stone
- Women discover the empty tomb
- Mary Magdalene sees the risen Lord
- Peter and John run to the tomb
- Jesus appears to two disciples on the road to Emmaus
- Jesus appears to the gathered disciples

## Word Studies

### Risen — ἐγείρω

<a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g1453/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="to awaken, raise up" data-strongs="G1453" data-translit="egeirō" data-original="ἐγείρω" data-lang="greek">ἐγείρω</a> (*egeirō*) — to awaken, raise up, arise

**The Angel's Declaration:**

> "He is not here: for he is risen, as he said."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/28?lang=eng&id=6" target="_blank">Matthew 28:6</a>

The Greek *egeirō* implies being awakened from sleep — the perfect metaphor for resurrection. Jesus Himself used this word when He said, "The third day I shall be raised again" (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/20?lang=eng&id=19" target="_blank">Matthew 20:19</a>).

---

### Touch Me Not — μή μου ἅπτου

<a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g680/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="to fasten, cling to" data-strongs="G680" data-translit="haptomai" data-original="ἅπτομαι" data-lang="greek">ἅπτομαι</a> (*haptomai*) — to fasten oneself to, cling to, touch

When Jesus said to Mary Magdalene "Touch me not" (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/20?lang=eng&id=17" target="_blank">John 20:17</a>), the Greek suggests "Stop clinging to me" — not a prohibition against any touch, but an invitation to release Him for His continuing mission.

Jesus had not yet ascended to the Father. The resurrected Lord still had sacred work to do.

## The First Witnesses

### Mary Magdalene

Mary Magdalene was the first person to see the risen Lord — a profound statement in a culture where women's testimony was not legally accepted.

> "Jesus first appears to women, the very same women who had personally witnessed the crucifixion and the burial, were now witnesses to the resurrection of the savior."

Mary had been healed of seven demons (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/8?lang=eng&id=2" target="_blank">Luke 8:2</a>) and became Christ's most devoted follower. Her transformation illustrates the power of redemption through faith.

### The Emmaus Disciples

Two disciples walking to Emmaus encountered a "stranger" who explained the scriptures to them. Only when He broke bread did they recognize Jesus — and He vanished.

> "Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way, and while he opened to us the scriptures?"
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/24?lang=eng&id=32" target="_blank">Luke 24:32</a>

## Theological Significance

### The Cornerstone of Christianity

> "If Christ be not risen, then is our preaching vain, and your faith is also vain."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-cor/15?lang=eng&id=14" target="_blank">1 Corinthians 15:14</a>

The resurrection is not merely an event — it is the foundation of all Christian hope. Without it, there is no salvation, no eternal life, no victory over death.

### The First Day

Jesus rose on "the first day of the week" — Sunday. This became the Christian day of worship, replacing the Jewish Sabbath (Saturday). Early Christians called it "the Lord's Day" (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/rev/1?lang=eng&id=10" target="_blank">Revelation 1:10</a>).

## Latter-day Saint Connections

### Book of Mormon Witness

The Book of Mormon provides additional testimony of the resurrection:

> "The spirit and the body shall be reunited again in its perfect form; both limb and joint shall be restored to its proper frame."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/11?lang=eng&id=43" target="_blank">Alma 11:43</a>

Christ appeared to the Nephites as a resurrected being, allowing them to feel the wounds in His hands and feet (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng&id=14-15" target="_blank">3 Nephi 11:14–15</a>).

### Modern Revelation

Joseph Smith testified of seeing the resurrected Christ:

> "We beheld the glory of the Son, on the right hand of the Father."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=20" target="_blank">D&C 76:20</a>

---

## Key Insight from Video Research

> "Be not faithless but believing."
> — Thomas's invitation from the risen Lord

Thomas's initial doubt became the occasion for one of the most powerful testimonies in scripture. When he finally saw Jesus, he declared: "My Lord and my God" (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/20?lang=eng&id=28" target="_blank">John 20:28</a>).

Jesus responded with a blessing for all believers: "Blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed."

## Reflection Questions

1. Mary Magdalene was the first witness. What does it mean that Jesus chose the marginalized to bear His most important testimony?

2. The Emmaus disciples recognized Jesus in "the breaking of bread." How do you recognize Christ's presence in your ordinances?

3. Thomas doubted but eventually believed. How has your faith grown through moments of uncertainty?
