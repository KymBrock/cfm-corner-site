## The Setting

**Date:** Friday–Saturday before Palm Sunday (8–9 Nisan)

**Location:** Bethany, about 2 miles east of Jerusalem on the Mount of Olives

**Key Events:**
- Jesus arrives in Bethany six days before Passover
- The raising of Lazarus (drawing crowds and attention)
- Mary of Bethany anoints Jesus' feet with costly spikenard
- Judas objects to the "waste" of the ointment
- Chief priests plot to kill both Jesus and Lazarus

## Word Studies

### Spikenard — נֵרְדְּ / νάρδος

<a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g3487/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="spikenard, nard" data-strongs="G3487" data-translit="nardos" data-original="νάρδος" data-lang="greek">νάρδος</a> (*nardos*) — from Hebrew <a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h5373/kjv/wlc/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="spikenard" data-strongs="H5373" data-translit="nerd" data-original="נֵרְדְּ" data-lang="hebrew">נֵרְדְּ</a> (*nerd*)

**Meaning:** A fragrant oil from the root of the spikenard plant, native to the Himalayas

The ointment Mary used was worth **300 denarii** — nearly a year's wages. This was no casual gift but an extravagant act of worship. The alabaster jar was likely a family heirloom, broken open for a single use.

### Anointing — χρίω

<a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5548/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="to anoint" data-strongs="G5548" data-translit="chriō" data-original="χρίω" data-lang="greek">χρίω</a> (*chriō*) — to anoint, to consecrate

This is the root of <a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g5547/kjv/tr/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="anointed, Messiah" data-strongs="G5547" data-translit="Christos" data-original="Χριστός" data-lang="greek">Χριστός</a> (*Christos*, "Christ") — "the Anointed One." The Hebrew equivalent is <a href="https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h4899/kjv/wlc/0-1/" target="_blank" data-lexicon="anointed, Messiah" data-strongs="H4899" data-translit="mashiach" data-original="מָשִׁיחַ" data-lang="hebrew">מָשִׁיחַ</a> (*mashiach*, "Messiah").

## The Three Anointed Offices

In ancient Israel, **three groups** were set apart through anointing with holy oil — **prophets, priests, and kings**. Anointing symbolized being consecrated for God's service, empowered by His Spirit.

### Prophet — נָבִיא

> "And Elisha the son of Shaphat... shalt thou **anoint to be prophet** in thy room."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-kgs/19?lang=eng&id=16" target="_blank">1 Kings 19:16</a>

**Purpose:** Prophets spoke God's word to the people, revealing His will and calling them to repentance.

**Jesus fulfills this:** Christ is "the Word made flesh" (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/1?lang=eng&id=14" target="_blank">John 1:14</a>), the ultimate revealer of the Father's will.

### Priest — כֹּהֵן

> "Take the anointing oil and **pour it on his head and anoint him**."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/29?lang=eng&id=7" target="_blank">Exodus 29:7</a>

**Purpose:** Priests interceded between God and the people, offering sacrifices for sin and entering God's presence on behalf of Israel.

**Jesus fulfills this:** Christ is our "great high priest" (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/4?lang=eng&id=14" target="_blank">Hebrews 4:14</a>) who entered the heavenly Holy of Holies with His own blood, interceding for us before the Father.

### King — מֶלֶךְ

> "And Samuel took the horn of oil, and **anointed him** in the midst of his brethren: and the Spirit of the LORD came upon David from that day forward."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-sam/16?lang=eng&id=13" target="_blank">1 Samuel 16:13</a>

**Purpose:** Kings ruled and protected God's people, establishing justice and leading them in righteousness.

**Jesus fulfills this:** Christ is "KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS" (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/rev/19?lang=eng&id=16" target="_blank">Revelation 19:16</a>), whose kingdom shall have no end.

## The Two Anointings of Holy Week

The Gospel accounts preserve **two anointings** during Holy Week, each with distinct symbolism:

### Mary's Anointing of Jesus' Feet (Saturday)

John places Mary's anointing the day before the Triumphal Entry — symbolically anointing Jesus as **King** before He rode into Jerusalem on a donkey, just as Solomon was anointed before riding David's mule to his coronation (<a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-kgs/1?lang=eng&id=33-39" target="_blank">1 Kings 1:33–39</a>).

### Unnamed Woman's Anointing of Jesus' Head (Wednesday)

Matthew and Mark place this anointing on Wednesday at Simon the Leper's house. By anointing Jesus' **head**, she symbolically anointed Him as the **great High Priest** who would soon enter God's presence — not with the blood of bulls and goats, but with His own blood.

Together, these women unknowingly testified that Jesus was both **King and Priest** — and through His teachings, also **Prophet**. Jesus is the only one who fulfills all three anointed offices.

---

## Theological Significance

### Mary's Prophetic Act

Mary of Bethany performed what Jesus called a "good work" — anointing Him for His burial before His death. While others missed the significance of what was coming, Mary seemed to understand, at some level, that Jesus would soon die.

> "Verily I say unto you, Wheresoever this gospel shall be preached throughout the whole world, this also that she hath done shall be spoken of for a memorial of her."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/14?lang=eng&id=9" target="_blank">Mark 14:9</a>

### Women as Witnesses

The anointing events highlight the role of women as powerful witnesses of Christ's divinity. In a patriarchal society, Jesus elevated and honored Mary's devotion, rebuking those who criticized her.

---

## Latter-day Saint Connections

### Temple Anointing

The anointing with oil connects to temple ordinances where members are anointed as part of sacred covenants. Just as Mary anointed Christ's body, temple participants are symbolically prepared for sacred purposes.

### Lazarus and Resurrection

The raising of Lazarus, occurring just days before Jesus' own death and resurrection, stands as a powerful witness that Jesus holds power over death — a power He would soon demonstrate for all humanity.

## Reflection Questions

1. Mary gave something of great value — a year's wages in ointment. What do you have to offer the Savior?

2. Judas saw "waste" where Jesus saw worship. How do we sometimes misjudge the devotion of others?

3. Jesus said Mary's act would be remembered "wherever the gospel is preached." Why did He honor her in this way?

## Key Insight from Video Research

> "In ancient times three main groups of people were anointed: prophets, priests, and kings. Both the words *Messiah* in Hebrew and *Christ* in Greek mean 'the Anointed One.'"
> — <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nhNZ_lVxJ2Q" target="_blank">Messages of Christ: The Anointing of Jesus by Women</a>

The anointing events foreshadow Jesus as **Prophet, Priest, and King** — the three offices set apart by anointing in ancient Israel. Mary's anointing of His feet (Saturday) symbolically anointed Him as King before His Triumphal Entry; the unnamed woman's anointing of His head (Wednesday) symbolically anointed Him as the great High Priest. These faithful women's acts highlight the role of women as powerful witnesses of Christ's divinity.
