## The Day

**Date:** Saturday, 16 Nisan (March/April) — The Sabbath

**Location:** Joseph's tomb, sealed and guarded

**Key Events:**
- Jesus' body lies in the tomb
- Roman soldiers guard the sealed tomb
- Disciples hide in fear and grief
- Women prepare spices for Sunday
- Christ's spirit ministers to the dead

## The Silence

Holy Saturday is often called "Silent Saturday" because the scriptures are largely silent about this day. The Gospels skip from Friday's burial to Sunday's resurrection.

Yet this silence speaks volumes:

- **For the disciples:** A day of crushing grief, shattered hopes, and paralyzing fear
- **For the women:** A day of waiting, preparing, and mourning
- **For the guards:** A routine assignment that would soon change history
- **For Christ:** Far from silent — He was actively at work

## Christ in the Spirit World

### Peter's Testimony

> "For Christ also hath once suffered for sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh, but quickened by the Spirit: By which also he went and preached unto the spirits in prison."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-pet/3?lang=eng&id=18-19" target="_blank">1 Peter 3:18–19</a>

### President Joseph F. Smith's Vision

In October 1918, President Joseph F. Smith received a revelation clarifying what happened during those hours when Christ's body lay in the tomb:

> "I perceived that the Lord went not in person among the wicked and the disobedient... but behold, from among the righteous, he organized his forces and appointed messengers, clothed with power and authority, and commissioned them to go forth and carry the light of the gospel to them that were in darkness."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/138?lang=eng&id=29-30" target="_blank">D&C 138:29–30</a>

## The Sealed Tomb

The chief priests and Pharisees remembered Jesus' prediction of rising after three days. They convinced Pilate to secure the tomb:

> "Command therefore that the sepulchre be made sure until the third day, lest his disciples come by night, and steal him away, and say unto the people, He is risen from the dead."
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/27?lang=eng&id=64" target="_blank">Matthew 27:64</a>

The irony: The very precautions meant to prevent fraud became the strongest evidence of the resurrection's authenticity.

## Theological Significance

### Three Days

Jewish reckoning counted any part of a day as a full day:
- **Friday** (before sunset) — Day 1
- **Saturday** (Sabbath) — Day 2
- **Sunday** (before dawn) — Day 3

Jesus fulfilled His prophecy of rising "the third day."

### The Sabbath Rest

There is deep symbolism in Christ resting in the tomb on the Sabbath. Just as God rested on the seventh day after creation, Christ rested after completing the work of redemption.

## Key Insight from Video Research

> "Only after sorrow can we feel joy; only after loss can we feel restoration."

Moments of despair are necessary for experiencing true joy and restoration afterward. Jesus' death and resurrection teach us about the power of overcoming sorrow through faith.

## Latter-day Saint Connections

### Temple Work for the Dead

The doctrine of Christ preaching to spirits in prison is foundational to Latter-day Saint temple work. During Holy Saturday, we remember that Christ's work extends beyond mortality — reaching all who have ever lived.

### Baptism for the Dead

> "Else what shall they do which are baptized for the dead, if the dead rise not at all? why are they then baptized for the dead?"
> — <a href="https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-cor/15?lang=eng&id=29" target="_blank">1 Corinthians 15:29</a>

## Reflection Questions

1. The disciples spent Saturday in despair, not knowing Sunday was coming. When have you been in a "Saturday" season of waiting?

2. While His body lay still, Christ's spirit was actively working. What does this teach about apparent inactivity in our lives?

3. Christ preached to spirits who had been waiting for centuries. What does this tell you about God's timeline versus ours?
