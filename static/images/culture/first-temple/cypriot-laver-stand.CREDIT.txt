Cypriot bronze four-wheeled cult-stand, Late Cypriot, c. 1050–950 BC, from Kition (Cyprus). Antikensammlung Berlin, inv. Misc. 8947. The closest known archaeological parallel to the wheeled laver-stands (mekonot) of Solomon's Temple (1 Kings 7:27–37).
