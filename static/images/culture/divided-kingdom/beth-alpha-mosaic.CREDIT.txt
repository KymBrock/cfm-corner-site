Mosaic floor of the Beth Alpha (Beit Alpha) synagogue, northern Israel, 6th century AD.
Three panels: the Torah shrine flanked by menorahs, lions, and birds (top); the zodiac
with Helios (center); the Binding of Isaac (bottom).
The mosaic is an ancient public-domain artwork; this is a faithful flat reproduction of a
2D public-domain work and carries no new copyright (public domain). Not an original CFM photo.
