Match the Holy Week and Passover Seder icons at:

---

## Base Prompt Template

```
Create a circular icon (perfect circle, no border).
Style: Abstract Corporate Memphis vector illustration.
Minimalist flat design. Paper cut-out aesthetic with
layered construction paper texture. Hard-edged geometric
shapes assembled like a collage. No edge outlines, minimal
internal lines, no sketchiness. Matte paper finish.
Drop shadows between paper layers to show depth.
Muted earthy color palette with mid saturation, flat fills, no gradients.
Output as PNG, 512x512px, transparent background outside the circle.

[SUBJECT DESCRIPTION]
Background color: [HEX COLOR]
```
