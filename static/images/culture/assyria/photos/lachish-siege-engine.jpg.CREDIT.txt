Assyrian siege-engine attacking the city wall of Lachish. The ascending and assaulting Assyrian military wave is overwhelming. Detail of a gypsum wall relief dating back to the reign of Sennacherib, 700-692 BCE. From the South-West Palace at Nineveh, Mesopotamia, modern-day Iraq, currently housed in the British Museum, London.
