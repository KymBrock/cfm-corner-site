## STYLE BLOCK — paste this first

> All images in this series are **layered paper-cut collage art**: every element is a separate piece of matte, softly textured construction paper, stacked in shallow layers with gentle drop shadows between them. Flat, hand-crafted, tactile — not photorealistic, not 3D-rendered, not glossy. Warm earth palette: ochre, sand, terracotta, muted gold, sage and olive green, dusty blue, soft clay red. Even, natural daylight. Calm and dignified in tone. **No text, no lettering, no letters or numbers of any kind, no captions, no watermarks, no borders or frames.** Historical subject matter must be accurate to ancient Assyria (c. 900–609 BC).

**Assyrian accuracy notes** (Assyria is *not* Babylon — keep these distinct):
- Landscape is **rain-fed rolling highland** on the upper Tigris, ringed by mountains — *not* the flat irrigated alluvium of southern Babylonia.
- Architecture uses **carved stone wall panels (orthostats)** and mudbrick — *not* Babylon's glazed blue brick.
- The king wears a **truncated conical crown with a small peak and a fillet band**; long square-cut curled beard; fringed shawl-robe.
- **Lamassu** = colossal human-headed *winged bull* (or lion) gate guardian, bearded, horned cap.
- **Ashur** appears as a **winged sun-disk**, often with an archer figure inside it.
- Soldiers wear **pointed/crested helmets**, scale armor; siege gear is wooden and leather.

### 05 — Writing & the Great Library → `05-hero.png`

### 06 — The Great Cities → `06-hero.png`

Center-right: **Dur-Sharrukin (Khorsabad)**, a fresh-built rectangular walled city with a **stepped tower** rising in receding tiers. Right: **Nineveh**, the largest — massive double walls, many-towered gates, and a terraced **royal garden** with trees and a watercourse. Mountains on the horizon; the river threading them all together.
