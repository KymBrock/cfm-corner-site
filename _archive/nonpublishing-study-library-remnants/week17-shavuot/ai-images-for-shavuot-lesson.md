# 🎨 Paper-Cut Illustration Style Guide (CFM Corner System)  
  
## Purpose  
This document defines the **locked visual style system** for all illustration packs:  
- Shavuot / Sinai  
- Sermon on the Mount  
- Third Nephi  
- Pentecost  
  
This ensures **consistent visual language** across all lesson materials.  
  
---  
  
# 🧱 Core Style Definition  
  
## STYLE REQUIREMENTS  
- Layered paper-cut composition (3–6 layers maximum)  
- Slight organic paper texture (subtle grain, not noisy)  
- Soft, rounded edges (NOT sharp vector edges)  
- Gentle shadow between layers (top-down lighting)  
- Slight natural irregularity in shapes (not perfectly symmetrical)  
  
---  
  
## 🎨 COLOR PALETTE  
  
Use warm, muted earth tones ONLY:  
  
- Wheat gold: `#d4a53e`  
- Clay brown: `#b1542f`  
- Soft cream: `#f5e6cc`  
- Olive green: `#6e7f5f`  
  
### Rules:  
- Slight tonal variation within shapes  
- No flat digital fills  
- No high saturation  
  
---  
  
## 💡 LIGHTING RULES  
  
- Soft, diffused overhead lighting  
- No glow effects  
- No light beams  
- No sparkles or particles  
- Depth created through layering ONLY  
  
---  
  
## 🌍 COMPOSITION RULES  
  
- All elements must feel **grounded**  
- Use layered earth/land forms as base  
- Include curved horizon layers  
- Maintain calm spacing and balance  
  
---  
  
## 👤 FIGURE STYLE  
  
- Simplified human figures  
- Rounded forms  
- No facial detail  
- Neutral tones  
- Symbolic poses:  
  - kneeling  
  - teaching  
  - reaching  
  - gathering  
  
---  
  
## 🔷 SYMBOL STYLE  
  
- Minimal and archetypal  
- Examples:  
  - flame  
  - scroll  
  - bread  
  - hands  
  - mountain  
- No intricate detail  
- No realism  
  
---  
  
## 🚫 DO NOT  
  
- Do NOT use sharp vector graphics  
- Do NOT use gradients  
- Do NOT add glow or effects  
- Do NOT add realism or textures beyond paper grain  
- Do NOT over-detail objects  
- Do NOT exaggerate proportions  
  
---  
  
## 🎯 GOAL  
  
All artwork should look like:  
  
> A handcrafted layered paper illustration photographed from above, with a calm and reverent tone.  
  
---  
  
# 🧩 Layout-Specific Prompts  
  
## HERO HEADER

## ICONS
